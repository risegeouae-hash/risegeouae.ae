# BIM mandates and requirements in MEA (research notes)

Status key: **Confirmed** = read on a primary or reputable secondary source; **Check** = reported but authority/number not verified. Verify everything before publishing on the site.

## United Arab Emirates – Dubai (Dubai Municipality)
- **Circular No. 196 (2013)** – Confirmed (secondary sources). BIM required for architectural and MEP work on buildings over 40 floors or over 300,000 sq ft, specialist buildings (hospitals, universities and similar), and projects by foreign engineering offices.
- **Circular No. 207 (2015)** – Confirmed (secondary sources). Threshold lowered to buildings over 20 floors or over 200,000 sq ft, plus all hospitals, universities, government projects and projects by foreign offices.
- **2023 circular, effective 1 January 2024** – Check (circular number not confirmed). Architectural BIM for buildings over 20 floors and structural BIM over 40 floors; complexes over 20,000 sq m (architectural) or 30,000 sq m (structural); specialised buildings; all government projects. It references the Dubai Building Code and the Dubai BIM standard.

## United Arab Emirates – Abu Dhabi
- Check. In 2019, MEED reported Abu Dhabi was considering a BIM mandate. No issued mandate was confirmed in this research round.

## Saudi Arabia
- Check. LRQA states BIM management is mandated for major projects from January 2024, but names no authority or circular number. Candidates to verify: Expro (Expenditure & Projects Efficiency Authority) and the Saudi BIM framework/roadmap, and Balady e-permit requirements.
