What is GeoAtlas MEA?

GeoAtlas MEA is a free directory of the geospatial industry in the Middle East and Africa. It lists 738 survey firms, GIS consultancies, BIM firms, equipment manufacturers and dealers, software vendors, agencies, universities and professional bodies in 70 countries, in 146 categories across four sections.

How do I find an accredited land surveyor in Dubai?

Dubai Land Department (DLD) publishes the survey companies accredited for real-estate and land surveys in Dubai, and those firms are listed here under Real Estate & Building Surveyors in the UAE. Confirm current accreditation on dubailand.gov.ae before appointing a surveyor.

Who regulates surveying and mapping in Saudi Arabia?

The General Authority for Survey and Geospatial Information (GEOSA) is Saudi Arabia's national survey and mapping authority. It maintains the national geodetic network and regulates surveying and geospatial activity in the Kingdom.
