Regulations, licensing and standards

Who licenses surveyors, who runs the cadastre and which BIM rules apply, country by country.

Survey licensing in the largest markets

CountryWho can sign a survey
United Arab EmiratesSurveying offices register with the land or municipal authority of each emirate. In Dubai, real estate surveyors who prepare unit and plot surveys for title registration work with the Dubai Land Department.
Saudi ArabiaEngineering and survey offices are licensed through the professional and municipal systems; the General Authority for Survey and Geospatial Information (GEOSA) is the national survey authority.
NigeriaSurveyors must be registered with the Surveyors Council of Nigeria (SURCON).
KenyaCadastral surveys must be signed by a surveyor licensed under the Survey Act.
South AfricaLand surveyors register with the South African Geomatics Council (SAGC).

Summarised from the authorities' public information. Always confirm the current rule with the authority. Full country pages (licensing, cadastre, drone permits and data rules) are being written one a week.
