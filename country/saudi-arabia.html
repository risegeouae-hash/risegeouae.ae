Questions

How many survey companies are listed in Saudi Arabia?

10 land or topographic survey firms are listed in Saudi Arabia, out of 39 geospatial organisations in total.

Which authorities regulate surveying and mapping in Saudi Arabia?

Listed authorities and bodies: Eastern Province Municipality (Saudi Arabia); General Authority for Survey and Geospatial Information (GEOSA); Saudi Council of Engineers. Confirm licensing rules with the authority before appointing a surveyor.

Where can I buy survey equipment in Saudi Arabia?

Listed manufacturers, distributors and dealers include Al Moryat Trading Company, Boksomatti, Tamstar Trading Est., SITECH KSA and GeoSystems.
