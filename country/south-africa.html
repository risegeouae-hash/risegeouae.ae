Questions

How many survey companies are listed in South Africa?

9 land or topographic survey firms are listed in South Africa, out of 46 geospatial organisations in total.

Which authorities regulate surveying and mapping in South Africa?

Listed authorities and bodies: South African Geomatics Council (SAGC); South African Geomatics Institute (SAGI); EIS Africa; National Geo-spatial Information (NGI). Confirm licensing rules with the authority before appointing a surveyor.

Where can I buy survey equipment in South Africa?

Listed manufacturers, distributors and dealers include Hexagon (Leica Geosystems) South Africa, Leica Geosystems SA, Kamarossi 22 (Pty) Ltd, Kazambu Consulting, Optron and Bendi 3D.
