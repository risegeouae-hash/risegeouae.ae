Questions

How many survey companies are listed in Nigeria?

13 land or topographic survey firms are listed in Nigeria, out of 62 geospatial organisations in total.

Which authorities regulate surveying and mapping in Nigeria?

Listed authorities and bodies: Abuja Geographic Information Systems (AGIS); GEONigeria; Nasarawa Geographic Information Service (NAGIS); Niger State Geographic Information System (NIGIS); Nigerian Institution of Surveyors; Office of the Surveyor-General of the Federation (OSGoF). Confirm licensing rules with the authority before appointing a surveyor.

Where can I buy survey equipment in Nigeria?

Listed manufacturers, distributors and dealers include Axiel Technologies Limited, Global Pro Services Limited (mamtus) and Industrial Commercial Projects Nigeria Limited (ICP).
