How many survey companies are listed in Kenya?

14 land or topographic survey firms are listed in Kenya, out of 48 geospatial organisations in total.

Which authorities regulate surveying and mapping in Kenya?

Listed authorities and bodies: Directorate of Resource Surveys and Remote Sensing (DRSRS); Engineers Board of Kenya; Institution of Surveyors of Kenya; Survey of Kenya. Confirm licensing rules with the authority before appointing a surveyor.

Where can I buy survey equipment in Kenya?

Listed manufacturers, distributors and dealers include Measurement Systems Limited, Achelis Kenya Limited, HiCAD Africa Limited and Geoid Technologies Ltd.
