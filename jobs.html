Questions

Where can I find survey and GIS jobs in the Middle East?

This page lists survey, GIS, BIM, drone and hydrographic vacancies posted by employers across the Middle East and Africa, and MEA Geospatial Jobs Weekly sends new ones every week. The directory also lists 483 organisations you can approach directly.

How do I find a GIS or surveying internship?

Apply to consultancies, mapping agencies and municipalities early in the academic year with a CV, a small portfolio and your university's placement letter. Internship Watch lists open internships every month.

Is posting a job free?

Yes. Employers post jobs and internships free while the board launches. Every post is checked with the employer before it is published.
