BIM mandates tracker: Middle East

Every government BIM requirement we know of, with an honest status. Nothing here is legal advice; confirm with the authority.

JurisdictionRequirementScopeStatus
United Arab Emirates – DubaiCircular widely reported as No. 196 (2013)BIM for architectural and MEP work on buildings over 40 floors or over 300,000 sq ft, specialist buildings such as hospitals and universities, and projects by foreign engineering offices.Reported by secondary sources; official copy being obtained
United Arab Emirates – DubaiCircular widely reported as No. 207 (2015)Thresholds lowered to buildings over 20 floors or over 200,000 sq ft, plus hospitals, universities, government projects and projects by foreign offices.Reported by secondary sources; official copy being obtained
United Arab Emirates – DubaiUpdated requirements effective 1 January 2024Architectural BIM above 20 floors and structural BIM above 40 floors; larger complexes and specialised buildings; all government projects; aligned with the Dubai Building Code.Circular number not verified
United Arab Emirates – Abu DhabiNo mandate confirmedA BIM mandate was reported as under consideration in 2019.Not confirmed
Saudi ArabiaMajor projects from January 2024 (reported)BIM management reported as required for major projects; the issuing authority and document were not identified.Not verified

Last reviewed 19 Nov 2026. If you hold an official copy of a circular, send it to us and we will verify the entry and credit you.
