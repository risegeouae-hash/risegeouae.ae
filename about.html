About GeoAtlas MEA

The geospatial industry directory for the Middle East and Africa, built to put every firm, supplier and survey company in the region in one place.

GeoAtlas MEA is an open directory of the geospatial industry across the GCC, the wider Middle East and North Africa, and Sub-Saharan Africa. It is free to search and free to be listed in.

What we include

Any organisation whose main work is geospatial: land and engineering survey, GIS, photogrammetry, remote sensing, hydrography, drone mapping, satellite imagery, survey equipment and software, plus the agencies and professional bodies that regulate and represent the profession.

Where the data comes from

Listings are compiled from public registers and directories, then supplemented by company submissions. Only business contact details that the organisation itself or a public authority has published are shown.

Sourced and Check labels

Sourced entries come from a published register or partner directory. Check entries come from general public information and have not yet been confirmed against the company's own website.

Corrections and removals

To correct, update or remove a listing, use the listing form or email info@risegeouae.ae.

Reuse the data

The dataset is available as CSV and JSON. Please credit GeoAtlas MEA with a link to https://risegeouae.ae.
