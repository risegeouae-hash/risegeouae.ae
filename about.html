About GeoAtlas MEA

The geospatial industry directory for the Middle East and Africa, built to put every firm, supplier, agency and university in the region in one place.

GeoAtlas MEA is an open directory of the geospatial industry across the GCC, the wider Middle East and all of Africa. It is free to search, free to be listed in, and its data is free to reuse.

What we include

Organisations whose main work is geospatial: land, engineering and hydrographic survey; drone, aerial and laser scanning; GIS, remote sensing and location intelligence; BIM and digital twins; survey equipment and software; and the agencies, universities, training centres, professional bodies and events around them. The directory has 146 categories in four sections. An organisation can appear in many categories.

Methodology

Listings are compiled from public registers, manufacturer partner and dealer pages, business directories and company submissions. Only business contact details published by the organisation or a public authority are shown. Equipment and software lists are ordered by role: manufacturers and regional offices first, then authorised distributors, then dealers and suppliers. Rankings, when published, will state their data sources, inclusion criteria and scoring.

Verified entries were confirmed on a named source page or on the organisation’s live official website. Unverified entries were found in a public source but their details are still to be confirmed; they are listed after verified entries.

Publisher

GeoAtlas MEA is published by Rise Geo Control Systems Trading LLC, a survey equipment supplier, rental and training company in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, and the UAE partner of the GeoTexon brand. Rise Geo and GeoTexon appear first in the lists they belong to. See the Rise Geo profile and the GeoTexon brand page.
