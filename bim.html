BIM Hub: ISO 19650, mandates and delivery in the Middle East & Africa

Professional guidance for clients, consultants, contractors and surveyors delivering BIM in the region, linked to the firms that do it.

Quick answer

BIM in the Middle East is delivered to ISO 19650: the client sets information requirements, the delivery team answers with a BIM execution plan, and all information flows through a common data environment. Dubai Municipality has required BIM on larger buildings since 2013, and Saudi Arabia's major projects increasingly ask for it.

Core topics

ISO 19650 information management

The international standard for managing information over an asset's life: OIR, AIR, PIR and EIR requirements, the BIM execution plan (BEP), MIDP and TIDP, and the CDE workflow.

BIM execution plan (BEP)

The delivery team's response to the client's exchange information requirements: roles, software, naming, LOD or level of information need, coordination and clash detection rules.

Level of development (LOD)

LOD 100 (concept) to LOD 500 (field-verified as-built) describes how far a model element can be relied on. Pair it with the level of information need for data and documents.

Common data environment (CDE)

One source of truth for drawings, models and documents, with status codes and revisions so everyone knows what can be used for construction.

4D and 5D BIM

Linking the model to the programme (4D) and to quantities and cost (5D) to simulate sequencing and control budgets.

BIM for facility management (6D/7D)

Handing over asset data (COBie or client templates) so operators can run and maintain the building from the model.

Scan-to-BIM

Using terrestrial or mobile laser scanning to capture existing conditions and model them, usually to LOD 300 or higher, for renovation, MEP coordination and as-built records.

BIM and GIS integration

Combining building models with GIS for campuses, utilities, roads and city digital twins, where survey control and coordinate systems must match.

Government BIM requirements

Mandates change and are often published as circulars that are hard to find. Our BIM mandates tracker lists each requirement with its status: confirmed, or still being verified against the official copy.

BIM questions

What is BIM?

Building information modelling (BIM) is the process of creating and managing a shared digital model of an asset, its geometry plus data, through design, construction and operation, so every party works from the same coordinated information.

What is the difference between LOD and level of information need?

LOD (level of development, LOD 100 to 500 in the BIMForum specification) describes how developed and reliable a model element is. ISO 19650 replaces loose LOD labels with the level of information need, which sets the geometry, data and documentation each deliverable must contain.

What is a common data environment (CDE)?

A CDE is the single agreed place where project information is stored, shared and approved. ISO 19650 defines four states: work in progress, shared, published and archived.

Is BIM mandatory in Dubai?

Dubai Municipality has required BIM on larger and specialist buildings since 2013, with thresholds updated since. Confirm the current circular and thresholds with Dubai Municipality; our mandates tracker shows what is verified.
