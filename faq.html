Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the directory and about finding geospatial services in the region.

What is GeoAtlas MEA?

GeoAtlas MEA is a free directory of the geospatial industry in the Middle East and Africa. It lists 483 survey firms, GIS consultancies, BIM firms, equipment manufacturers and dealers, software vendors, agencies, universities and professional bodies in 49 countries, in 146 categories across four sections.

How do I find an accredited land surveyor in Dubai?

Dubai Land Department (DLD) publishes the survey companies accredited for real-estate and land surveys in Dubai, and those firms are listed here under Real Estate & Building Surveyors in the UAE. Confirm current accreditation on dubailand.gov.ae before appointing a surveyor.

Who regulates surveying and mapping in Saudi Arabia?

The General Authority for Survey and Geospatial Information (GEOSA) is Saudi Arabia's national survey and mapping authority. It maintains the national geodetic network and regulates surveying and geospatial activity in the Kingdom.

Where can I buy GNSS receivers, total stations and laser scanners in the Middle East and Africa?

Each brand page lists its regional offices, authorised distributors and dealers by country; for example Leica Geosystems, Trimble, Topcon and Esri. Start at the Brands page or Products & Equipment.

Why do manufacturers appear first in equipment lists?

Equipment and software lists follow one rule for everyone: manufacturers and regional offices first, then authorised distributors, then dealers, suppliers, rental and repair. Placement is never sold.

How do I list or claim my company?

Listing is free. Use List your company for a new listing or Claim your profile to update an existing one. We check every submission before publishing.

What do the Sourced and Check labels mean?

Sourced means the entry was taken from a published register or official partner list. Check means the entry comes from general public information and has not yet been confirmed with the organisation.

Can I download the directory data?

Yes. The dataset is open as CSV and JSON on the Research page under CC BY 4.0. Please credit GeoAtlas MEA with a link.
