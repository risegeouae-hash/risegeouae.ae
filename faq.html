Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about the directory and about finding geospatial services in the region.

What is GeoAtlas MEA?

GeoAtlas MEA is a free directory of the geospatial industry in the Middle East and Africa. It lists 110 survey companies, GIS consultancies, equipment and software suppliers, drone and satellite operators, mapping agencies and professional bodies across 16 countries, searchable by country, category and service.

How do I find an accredited land surveyor in Dubai?

Dubai Land Department (DLD) publishes the list of survey companies accredited for real-estate and land surveys in Dubai. All 12 DLD-accredited firms are listed in GeoAtlas MEA under United Arab Emirates, with the phone numbers DLD publishes. Always confirm current accreditation on dubailand.gov.ae before appointing a surveyor.

Who regulates surveying and mapping in Saudi Arabia?

The General Authority for Survey and Geospatial Information (GEOSA), formerly GASGI, is Saudi Arabia's national survey and mapping authority. It maintains the national geodetic network and licenses surveying and geospatial activity in the Kingdom.

What is the difference between a survey company and a GIS company?

A survey company measures the real world in the field: boundaries, levels, buildings, utilities and the seabed, using GNSS, total stations, laser scanners and drones. A GIS company manages and analyses that spatial data, building maps, databases, dashboards and digital twins. Many firms in the region do both.

Where can I buy GNSS receivers, total stations and laser scanners in the Middle East?

Major manufacturers such as Trimble, Leica Geosystems, Topcon, CHC Navigation, Stonex, RIEGL and FARO sell through regional offices and authorised dealers. See the Hardware Supplier category for distributors by country.

Which organisations run Earth observation satellites in the region?

Government operators include the Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Centre (UAE), the Egyptian Space Agency, NASRDA (Nigeria) and SANSA (South Africa). Commercial operators and imagery providers include Space42, Neo Space Group, SARsatX, Space Imaging Middle East and Amaya Space.

How do I list my company in the directory?

Listing is free. Fill in the form on the List your company page with your company name, country, category, services and website. Each submission is checked against the company website before it is published.

What do the Sourced and Check labels mean?

Sourced means the entry was taken from a published list such as a government accreditation register or an official partner directory. Check means the entry comes from general public information and its details have not yet been confirmed against the company's own website.

Can I download the directory data?

Yes. The full dataset is available as CSV and JSON from the links in the site footer, so researchers and AI tools can cite it. Please credit GeoAtlas MEA when you reuse it.
