State of MEA geospatial 2026: the directory in numbers

Original data on where the region's survey, GIS, BIM and geospatial organisations are, and what they do.

GeoAtlas MEA lists 483 geospatial organisations in 49 of the 70 Middle East and African countries. The United Arab Emirates has the most listings (78), followed by Nigeria (53), Kenya (46) and South Africa (37). Land surveyors (86) and GIS consultants (82) are the largest categories.

Source: GeoAtlas MEA directory, 19 Nov 2026. Counts include each organisation once per section or category it appears in.
