Survey Equipment Sales & Technical Support Jobs in the Middle East & Africa

Land surveyorGISBIMDroneHydrographicRemote sensingEquipment salesInternships
Quick answer

Survey equipment distributors and dealers hire sales engineers, product specialists and service technicians. These roles need hands-on knowledge of GNSS, total stations, scanners or drones, the ability to train customers, and for service roles, calibration and repair skills.

Vacancies

No vacancies posted here yet. Employers post free, and every vacancy appears in the weekly jobs roundup. Post a job

What the job involves

Demonstrate and sell instruments and software, train customers, provide technical support, and calibrate and repair equipment.

Skills and qualifications employers ask for

  • Field experience with GNSS, total stations, scanners or drones
  • Customer training and demonstrations
  • Calibration and repair (service roles)
  • Knowledge of survey and CAD software
  • Sales or account management experience
