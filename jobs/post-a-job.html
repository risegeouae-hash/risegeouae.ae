Post a job or internship, free

Reach surveyors, GIS analysts, BIM professionals and graduates across the Middle East and Africa. We check every post with the employer before publishing.

Open email


How posting works
  1. Send the form, from your company email if you can.
  2. We confirm the vacancy with you and publish it on the jobs pages, with Google-ready job markup so it can appear in Google job search.
  3. It goes out in MEA Geospatial Jobs Weekly and is removed after the closing date.
