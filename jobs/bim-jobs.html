BIM Modeller, Coordinator & Manager Jobs in the Middle East & Africa

Land surveyorGISBIMDroneHydrographicRemote sensingEquipment salesInternships
Quick answer

BIM jobs in the Gulf follow a ladder from BIM modeller to BIM coordinator to BIM manager. Employers ask for Revit and Navisworks skills, clash detection and coordination experience, and for senior roles a working knowledge of ISO 19650, BIM execution plans and common data environments.

Vacancies

No vacancies posted here yet. Employers post free, and every vacancy appears in the weekly jobs roundup. Post a job

What the job involves

Model buildings and infrastructure, coordinate disciplines and resolve clashes, manage the common data environment, write and enforce BIM execution plans, and prepare models for authority submission and handover.

Skills and qualifications employers ask for

  • Autodesk Revit, Navisworks and AutoCAD
  • Clash detection and MEP coordination
  • ISO 19650, BEP, EIR and CDE workflows (coordinator and manager roles)
  • Tekla, Archicad or civil BIM tools for specialist roles
  • A degree or diploma in architecture, engineering or construction
