Land Surveyor & Survey Engineer Jobs in the Middle East & Africa

Land surveyorGISBIMDroneHydrographicRemote sensingEquipment salesInternships
Quick answer

Land surveyor and survey engineer jobs in the Middle East and Africa usually ask for a diploma or degree in surveying or geomatics, field experience with GNSS and total stations, CAD skills, and for signing cadastral work, registration with the national surveying authority or council.

What the job involves

Set out buildings and roads, run topographic, cadastral and as-built surveys, process field data into CAD drawings and survey plans, and check work against the project's coordinate system and tolerances.

Skills and qualifications employers ask for

  • Degree or diploma in surveying, geomatics or civil engineering
  • GNSS (RTK) and total station field work
  • AutoCAD Civil 3D or similar survey CAD
  • Local coordinate systems and datums
  • For cadastral work: registration with the national survey authority or council (for example SURCON in Nigeria or SAGC in South Africa)
