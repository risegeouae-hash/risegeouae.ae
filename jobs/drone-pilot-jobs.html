Drone Pilot & Photogrammetry Jobs in the Middle East & Africa

Land surveyorGISBIMDroneHydrographicRemote sensingEquipment salesInternships
Quick answer

Drone survey jobs combine flying and data processing: a remote pilot licence from the national civil aviation authority, mapping-grade flight planning, ground control with GNSS, and processing in photogrammetry or LiDAR software into orthophotos, surface models and point clouds.

Vacancies

No vacancies posted here yet. Employers post free, and every vacancy appears in the weekly jobs roundup. Post a job

What the job involves

Plan and fly mapping and inspection missions, set ground control points, process imagery into orthophotos, DSMs and point clouds, and deliver volumes, contours and inspection reports.

Skills and qualifications employers ask for

  • A remote pilot licence or permit from the national civil aviation authority
  • Flight planning for mapping and inspection
  • Photogrammetry software such as Pix4D or Agisoft Metashape
  • GNSS ground control (RTK/PPK)
  • Point cloud and GIS basics
