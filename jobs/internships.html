Geospatial Internships & Graduate Programmes in the Middle East & Africa

Land surveyorGISBIMDroneHydrographicRemote sensingEquipment salesInternships
Quick answer

Survey, GIS and BIM internships in the Middle East and Africa are usually offered by consultancies, mapping agencies, municipalities and equipment dealers, often tied to university placement schemes. Apply early in the academic year with a CV, a short portfolio of maps or models, and your university's placement letter.

Vacancies

No vacancies posted here yet. Employers post free, and every vacancy appears in the weekly jobs roundup. Post a job

What the job involves

Support field surveys, data capture and digitising, map production, BIM modelling and equipment demonstrations under supervision, usually for two to six months.

Skills and qualifications employers ask for

  • Enrolment in, or recent graduation from, surveying, GIS, geomatics, architecture or engineering
  • A portfolio: maps, models or a small project
  • Basic ArcGIS/QGIS, AutoCAD or Revit
  • A placement letter from your university, where required

Enrolment in, or recent graduation from, surveying, GIS, geomatics, architecture or engineering; A portfolio: maps, models or a small project; Basic ArcGIS/QGIS, AutoCAD or Revit.
