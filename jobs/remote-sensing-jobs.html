Quick answer

Remote sensing jobs in the region sit with space agencies, Earth observation companies and GIS consultancies. They ask for satellite image processing, classification and change detection, often with Python, cloud platforms and increasingly machine learning for feature extraction.

Vacancies

No vacancies posted here yet. Employers post free, and every vacancy appears in the weekly jobs roundup. Post a job

What the job involves

Process optical and SAR satellite imagery, classify land cover, detect change, monitor crops, environment and infrastructure, and turn imagery into maps and indicators.

Skills and qualifications employers ask for

  • Optical and SAR image processing
  • Classification and change detection
  • Python with geospatial libraries
  • Cloud EO platforms
  • A degree in remote sensing, geomatics, geography or physics
