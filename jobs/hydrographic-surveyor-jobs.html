Hydrographic Surveyor Jobs in the Middle East & Africa

Land surveyorGISBIMDroneHydrographicRemote sensingEquipment salesInternships
Quick answer

Hydrographic surveyors map the seabed, ports and waterways with single- and multibeam echo sounders and marine positioning. Many employers prefer qualifications from programmes recognised by the international FIG/IHO/ICA standards board (Category A or B), plus offshore safety certificates for vessel work.

What the job involves

Run bathymetric, dredging and offshore site surveys, operate echo sounders and positioning systems, process and clean sonar data, and produce charts and volume reports.

Skills and qualifications employers ask for

  • Multibeam and single-beam echo sounder operation
  • Marine GNSS and motion sensors
  • Processing software for hydrographic data
  • Category A or B hydrographic training (preferred)
  • Offshore safety and sea survival certificates for vessel work
